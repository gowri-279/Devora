# Reference Notes

The brief contains two embedded Bob illustrations. Bob is a compact friendly robot mascot with a rounded white face, simple black oval eyes, a small smile, dark outline, blue-purple hard hat, blue shoes, and a code-bracket badge on the chest. The strongest reference for the landing hero is the second image: Bob is seated/standing beside a laptop with one hand raised in a wave. The artwork style is clean vector/cartoon with cool blue and violet accents on a white background.
